1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

The LaserJet Pro X500 is a high-performance multifunction printer designed for
small to medium-sized offices. It supports print, scan, copy, and fax operations
with Wi-Fi and USB connectivity.

Key Specifications:
- Print Speed: 40 ppm (black), 30 ppm (color)
- Resolution: 1200 x 1200 dpi
- Max Paper Size: A3
- Memory: 512 MB RAM
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 6, USB 3.0, Ethernet

2. SETUP AND INSTALLATION
================================================================================

2.1 Unboxing

Before setup, ensure the following items are present in the box:
- LaserJet Pro X500 printer unit
- Power cable
- USB cable
- Starter ink cartridges (CYMK set)
- Quick start guide
- Warranty card

2.2 Installing Ink Cartridges

Follow these steps to install the ink cartridges:
- Open the front access door by pressing the release button
- Pull the cartridge tray outward until it clicks
- Remove protective tape from each cartridge
- Insert each cartridge into the matching color slot (C=Cyan, Y=Yellow, M=Magenta, K=Black)
- Push each cartridge until it clicks into place
- Close the front door
- Wait for the alignment page to print automatically

2.3 Connecting to Wi-Fi

Steps to connect the printer to a wireless network:
- Press the Network button on the control panel
- Select Wireless Setup Wizard
- Choose your Wi-Fi network from the list
- Enter the Wi-Fi password using the touchscreen keyboard
- Press Connect and wait for confirmation
- Print a Network Configuration Page to confirm

3.2 Resolving Error E05 — Paper Jam

Paper jams are the most common printer issue. They occur when paper feeds
incorrectly or tears inside the mechanism.

Causes of paper jams:
- Using wrinkled, wet, or incompatible paper
- Overloaded paper tray
- Worn or dirty paper feed rollers
- Incorrect paper size settings

Step-by-step resolution for E05:
- Step 1: Turn off the printer using the power button
- Step 2: Open ALL access doors — front, rear, and top
- Step 3: Locate the jammed paper. Never pull forcefully.
- Step 4: Gently pull the paper in the direction of paper travel (downward)
- Step 5: Check for torn pieces — even small pieces can cause repeat jams
- Step 6: Clean the rollers with a slightly damp lint-free cloth
- Step 7: Close all doors firmly
- Step 8: Reload paper correctly (do not overfill)
- Step 9: Power on the printer
- Step 10: Send a test print to confirm resolution

If the jam recurs more than 3 times in one hour, contact technical support.

3.3 Resolving Error E01 — Low Ink Warning

The printer detects that one or more ink levels are critically low.

Recommended cartridge replacement procedure:
- Purchase only OEM LaserJet Pro X500 cartridges
- Avoid third-party refills which may void warranty
- Replace the indicated cartridge immediately
- Run a printhead alignment after replacement
- Dispose of old cartridges using the included recycling label

3.4 Resolving Error E12 — Network Timeout

The printer has lost connection to the local network.

Troubleshooting steps:
- Verify that the router is powered on and functioning
- Check that the printer is within 15 meters of the router
- Restart both the router and printer
- Re-run the Wireless Setup Wizard
- If using Ethernet, verify the cable is securely connected
- Disable VPN on the host computer temporarily
- Update printer firmware via the settings menu

4. MAINTENANCE
================================================================================

4.1 Cleaning the Printhead

Regular printhead cleaning extends print quality:
- Access Settings > Maintenance > Clean Printhead
- The process takes approximately 3-5 minutes
- Do not open covers during cleaning
- Run up to 3 cleaning cycles if quality remains poor
- If still poor after 3 cycles, replace the cartridge

4.2 Replacing Paper Feed Rollers

Feed rollers should be replaced every 100,000 pages or when:
- Paper frequently misfeed or jams
- Paper picks multiple sheets at once
- Rollers appear worn or glazed

Roller replacement procedure:
- Power off and unplug the printer
- Open the rear access panel
- Press the release tabs on both ends of the roller assembly
- Slide the old roller off the spindle
- Slide the new roller onto the spindle until it clicks
- Close the rear panel and power on

4.3 Calibration and Alignment

Run calibration when:
- Colors appear shifted or misaligned
- After cartridge replacement
- After firmware update

Calibration procedure:
- Go to Settings > Print Quality > Full Calibration
- Load 5 sheets of plain white A4 paper
- Press Start Calibration
- The printer will print and scan calibration patterns
- Process takes 8-12 minutes

6. WARRANTY AND SUPPORT
================================================================================

6.1 Warranty Terms

The LaserJet Pro X500 comes with a 3-year limited warranty covering:
- Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship
- Hardware failures under normal operating conditions
- Free replacement of defective parts within warranty period

The warranty does NOT cover:
- Physical damage caused by misuse or accidents
- Damage from third-party ink cartridges
- Normal wear items (rollers, belts, fusers)
- Damage from power surges

6.2 Contacting Technical Support

Support channels:
- Online chat: support.laserjet.com/chat (24/7)
- Phone: 1-800-PRINTER (Mon-Fri 8am-8pm EST)
- Email: support@laserjet-pro.com (response within 24 hours)
- Local service center: Find at support.laserjet.com/locations

Before contacting support, have ready:
- Printer model and serial number (on label under printer)
- Error code displayed on the screen
- Description of the issue and when it started
- Number of pages printed (from Settings > Printer Info)
